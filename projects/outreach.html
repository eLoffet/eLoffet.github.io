CURIEuses

october 2025

I had the opportunity to participate in 'CURIE-euses au dela des frontières' , an exhibition by D-Fi USA highlighting francophone female scientists working in New York City.

With the other -very talented- women of the exhibition. Picture by Adelaide Bacon

The exhibition was held at the Maison de l'Occitanie, in Manhattan. We hosted kids from local schools to talk about our research and answer questions about being a scientist. The exhibition was also held at a couple schools in NYC. Find out more about the exhibition here.
