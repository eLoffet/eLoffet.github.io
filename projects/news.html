CURIEuses

october 2025

I had the great honor to participate in 'CURIE-euses au dela des frontières' , an exhibition by D-Fi USA highlighting francophone female scientists working in New York City.

With the other -very talented- women of the exhibition for the booklet picture. Picture by Adelaide Bacon
The portraits at PS 84 school

The exhibition was held at the Maison de l'Occitanie, in Manhattan, and inaugurated with a panel discussion about being a woman in science. The following week, we hosted kids from local schools to talk about our research and answer questions about being a scientist there. The exhibition was also held at PS 84 elementay school and the Bronx High school of Science. Find out more about the exhibition here.
